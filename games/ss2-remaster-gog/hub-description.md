# System Shock 2: 25th Anniversary Remaster — GOG / 2 gamepads

[Source code, updates and full documentation on GitHub](https://github.com/alex-nax/coop/tree/main/games/ss2-remaster-gog)

Initial community handler for GOG 1.3 (build 89551), tested with Nucleus Co-op 2.4.2. Two XInput gamepads, recommended top/bottom split. Human testing confirmed launch, independent controllers and LAN connection.

Assign one controller to each half and press Play. In both windows choose Multiplayer > LAN; host on one side and join 127.0.0.1 on the other. Saves are separate under NucleusCo-op\players\SS2RemasterGOG\Player1 and Player2. Keep both pads awake through startup. No extra multiplayer emulator is required.

Made with AI assistance using OpenAI Codex, with human testing. Unofficial community work; no affiliation with SplitScreen.Me, GOG or the publisher, and no claim of official Hub verification. Requires your own game. Long campaign progression/save-reload, other builds and additional players remain unverified.
